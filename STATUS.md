## Go-live gate

Before enabling `DropboxSignProvider` outside local and CI, confirm each signed or declined webhook by fetching the signature request from the Dropbox Sign API and comparing it to the payload. Dropbox Sign's `event_hash` is an HMAC of `event_time` and `event_type` only. It does not cover the body, so a matching hash does not prove the workspace id, the proposal id, or the signer list. ADR 0021. The adapter does not perform that fetch yet. Leave `DROPBOX_SIGN_API_KEY` empty until it does.

## Not in this slice

- Work-order transitions after `ready`. This slice only inserts `ready`.
- A proposal-number revision suffix. Spec 08 names one and does not define it. See `docs/specs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.
- A live Dropbox Sign account, and the fetch that confirms a signed or declined webhook against the Dropbox Sign API. See the go-live gate. `DROPBOX_SIGN_API_KEY` stays empty in local and CI.
- Change orders, deposits, and a client portal beyond the public proposal page.
- v1 stages (`scheduled` through `closed`) are in the check constraint and are not reachable.
- A foreign key from `takeoff_item` to `job` or `location`. Those tables exist in `0005`. `0006` does not reference them.
- A saved draft total matches `previewRecipe`. A ready or sent estimate serves its snapshot. Cloning a draft does not supersede it. `accepted` is not superseded, because the state machine does not allow it.
- Assemblies of assemblies, price lists, and vendor feeds.
- `output_unit`. The column is `input_unit`. The entity table still says `output_unit`. See `docs/specs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.
- A county boundary table. A county FIPS is accepted, and the raster footprint is not checked against a county polygon.
- A live NC or NAIP download in CI. The fetcher exists and stays off.
- A PNG that composites aerial imagery. ADR 0017 stamps provider, date, GSD, and attribution on a schematic of the measurements. ADR 0020 embeds that PNG in the proposal PDF and repeats the credit as text. Follow-up: neither the export nor the PDF reads COG pixels.
- Parcel search stays capped at 50 rows within 2 km of the site. A wider view can omit lots. Follow-up, not a blocker for this slice.
- Measurement tiles throttle at 30 calls per 10 seconds per workspace per API process. Two processes do not share a counter. Follow-up.
- Snap can pull a hole onto the outer ring, and the hole is then rejected. Follow-up. The editor test turns snap off before cutting.
- `sheet_geom`, `planset_sheet`, and plan-set scale. Spec 12 owns those. A measurement in this slice is geographic.
- Hole cut and undo/redo on an accepted assist polygon. Those tools are the map takeoff follow-up and are not on main. Vertex edit and the server summary are covered here. AI auto drawing is still later.
- Custom roles, SSO, SCIM, and per-field permissions beyond cost visibility.
- A browser-facing signed tile URL. Imagery still proxies.
- Marking a task done. The API stores `done` and this screen does not change it.
- A signed download that outlives the 900 second presign. See `docs/specs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.
- Sentry. Choosing a license. Choosing between Fly and Render.
- The remaining gaps in `docs/specs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.
- Sign-ups or spend. No hosted secrets in git. Local compose passwords, the tile signing secret, and the local Better Auth secret are laptop defaults and are refused when `NODE_ENV=production`.
